ABOUT GUNINA HOLIDAYS

Where Dreams Meet Their Real Destination

Thoughtful planning. Personal attention. Journeys shaped around you.

Gunina Holidays brings together holiday planning, travel bookings and documentation assistance to help you turn an idea into a well-organised journey. Whether you are planning a family holiday, a honeymoon, a group getaway or a business visit, we start with what matters to you: your interests, travel dates, comfort and budget.

A journey that feels like yours

We believe a memorable holiday begins with understanding the traveller. Some journeys call for time to unwind; others are built around culture, nature, city experiences or spending time together. We help you choose destinations, build practical routes and select travel arrangements that suit your preferred pace.

Our approach connects the details that make a trip work: flights, accommodation, transfers, sightseeing and the documents you need to prepare. Rather than treating each booking as a separate task, we help you see how the arrangements fit into your complete travel plan.

Complete travel planning, in one place

Our services include customised holiday packages, flight and hotel booking assistance, visa documentation support, passport renewal assistance and travel insurance assistance. We also help plan group tour packages and MICE requirements, including meetings, incentives, conferences and exhibitions, according to the scope of your enquiry.

From a short city break to a multi-destination holiday, we help you compare options and understand the proposed itinerary before you confirm. Your quotation sets out the selected arrangements, inclusions and applicable booking conditions so you can make an informed decision.

Our approach to your holiday

Listen first

Tell us who is travelling, what you enjoy and how you want to spend your time. Your preferences guide the plan.

Plan thoughtfully

We consider travel time, hotel locations and sightseeing priorities to build a route with a comfortable balance.

Keep things clear

We explain proposed arrangements, documentation needs and booking details before you proceed.

Support for different ways to travel

Families, couples, individual travellers and groups have different needs. We help you shape the itinerary around those differences, whether that means relaxed leisure days, shared group experiences, well-located accommodation or a combination of business and leisure arrangements.

Documentation with care

Visa and passport processes can involve several steps. We assist with preparing and organising the relevant documents for your enquiry and explain the information needed for application preparation. Decisions and processing timelines remain with the issuing authority.

Let us plan your next journey

Share your destination ideas, dates, number of travellers and approximate budget. Gunina Holidays will help you explore suitable options and build a travel plan around your requirements.
